# Authentication food backdrop

`food-background.jpg` is an original image generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Campusbite's authentication screens, encoded as JPEG at its original dimensions to reduce download size. The interface, branding, curve and text are rendered by the app, separately from the photograph. The photograph is decorative and does not represent the live menu.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: food photography background for a campus canteen login UI, not a UI mockup. Create one high-resolution landscape 3:2 photograph, full bleed, overhead flat lay of an appetizing Filipino campus lunch spread on a dark warm charcoal stone tabletop. Beautiful realistic ceramic plates: glossy chicken adobo with steamed white rice and a sunny-side egg near the upper left, golden fried chicken and rice toward the lower right, a bowl of pancit noodles toward the upper right, a small glass of amber iced tea near the lower left. Loose calamansi halves, tomato slices, a few green herbs and a softly rumpled natural beige linen napkin around the outer edges. Rich warm burnt orange, caramel, cream and natural fresh green accents. Art-directed editorial food photography, soft directional window light, convincing food textures, deep soft shadows, cozy modern cafe mood. Arrange generously cropped dishes around the edges, leaving a little dark quiet tabletop near the center and lower center for interface overlays. Food should feel plentiful and inviting, not clinical. No text, no lettering, no branding, no watermark, no people, no phones, no interface, no decorative borders. Save the image as a reusable project asset.
